The exam, in plain terms

IELTS (International English Language Testing System) scores your English across four skills, each banded from 0–9. Universities, employers, and immigration authorities read that single number to gauge how you'll cope in English day to day.

Listening

~30 min + transfer time

4 recordings, 40 questions, each section harder than the last.

Reading

60 minutes

3 passages (Academic) or varied texts (General Training), 40 questions.

Writing

60 minutes

2 tasks — a report or letter, then a longer essay.

Speaking

11–14 minutes

A face-to-face interview in three parts, with an examiner.

Academic

For university admission or professional registration. Reading and Writing use academic-style passages and a data-report task.

General Training

For migration or work experience. Reading and Writing use everyday, workplace-style texts and a letter task.

The band scale

Each skill is banded 0–9 in half-band steps; your overall band is the rounded average of all four.

9.0Expert user — fully operational command of English
8.0Very good user — occasional inaccuracies in unfamiliar situations
7.0Good user — occasional errors, handles complex language well
6.0Competent user — generally effective command despite some errors
5.0Modest user — partial command, copes with overall meaning
4.0Limited user — basic competence in familiar situations only
