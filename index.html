About IELTS

The International English Language Testing System (IELTS) is the world’s most popular English language proficiency test for higher education and global migration. It assesses your abilities in listening, reading, writing and speaking.

This platform provides you with high‑quality study materials, a score tracker, and an AI‑powered feedback module to help you identify weaknesses and improve your band score.

Test Format

  • Listening – 30 minutes
  • Reading – 60 minutes
  • Writing – 60 minutes
  • Speaking – 11–14 minutes

Band Score

Scores are reported in whole and half bands, from 1 (non‑user) to 9 (expert user).
